## 2026-09-15–2026-09-17 — Deliver the optimization and local execution slice

**Outcome:** P3.1–P3.5 added the mathematical formulation, deterministic benchmarks, bounded exact enumeration, comparable strategy results, and configurable route/time/capacity diagnostics. The small fixture improved from heuristic cost **792** to enumeration cost **744**. This was the limited enumerator, not the native OR-Tools integration delivered later.

P3.6–P3.8 added a local JSON history repository, an HTTP API, and queued lifecycle states with retries, duration checks, and concurrency settings. P3.9–P3.12 added fixed-plan Monte Carlo simulation, VaR/CVaR comparison, the local scenario console, Docker/Compose, CI, and the independent demo command.

**Limits:** The queue is in process and memory; persisted results do not imply durable pending-job recovery. Native solver isolation and transactional shared storage were not delivered here. Required-order annotations describe diagnostics rather than forcing service of every annotated demand.

## 2026-09-29 — Add inference, decision loss, backtesting, and sensitivity

**Outcome:** S3.3–S3.6 introduced paired strategy comparisons, cost-delta intervals, CVaR bootstrap intervals, decision profiles, temporal backtesting, regret, and sensitivity to priors/distributions/penalties. These methods evaluate the declared scenarios and decision preferences; they do not establish universal strategy superiority.
