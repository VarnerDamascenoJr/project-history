## 2026-09-24 — Establish the Sales analytics product

**Outcome:** RD1 introduced the `sales-analytics-export.v1` types, versioned fixture, and pure transformers. RD2 added the optional Sales API client, `X-API-Key` header, minimal schema validation, readable failures, and fixture fallback. RD3 replaced the administrative Home with operational KPIs, time-window and event-distribution charts, funnel/survival views, and an event table. RD4 added browser-side JSON, event/window CSV, and chart PNG exports.

**Decision:** Keep the default experience fixture-first and deterministic. The Sales service owns data production; the browser displays and exports the contract without claiming new statistical conclusions.
